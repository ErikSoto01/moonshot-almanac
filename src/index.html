About

How Moonshot Almanac works

Moonshot Almanac is an independent Facebook Page for people who'd like to know what's up there tonight, and the true stories behind it. It's made for U.S. skies and U.S. times.

Faceless by design
There's no host on camera. The graphics, star charts and animations are made for the Page, and the sky positions are computed, not guessed.
Sources on every post
Captions name where each fact comes from, usually NASA, the Smithsonian, or the observatory behind the result. Sky times are calculated with Astronomy Engine and checked against published almanacs.
AI narration, disclosed
Our videos are narrated by an AI voice, and every Reel carries Facebook's AI label. A person researches and checks each script before anything is posted.
Calm, no hype
We don't call things "rare" or "once in a lifetime" unless they are, and we're honest about moonlight and clouds.
Corrections
If we get something wrong, we fix the caption and pin a comment that says what changed. We never delete a mistake silently. Spotted one? Send the Page a message.

Privacy

This site has no cookies, no analytics, no ads and no trackers. Its fonts and scripts load from this site, not from other companies.
